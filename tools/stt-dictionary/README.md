`corpus_review.py` inventories private raw-text archives and screens sentences through the existing local SystemOne choice endpoint. Its receipts distinguish `clean`, `suspect` and `uncertain`; none proves what was spoken. All nonclean choices enter the private queue, including low-confidence cases. Unique-text and sentence coverage are counted separately and malformed/duplicate summary receipts are rejected. The earlier scalar pilot is invalid and excluded. This utility never activates aliases or rewrites transcripts; semantic screening and direct context review may still be in progress. See [mining scope](mining-spec.md).
